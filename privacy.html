WaitWise Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Single Purpose

WaitWise is a browser extension created to monetize the idle waiting period while Large Language Models (ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini) generate responses. When a user sends a prompt, WaitWise displays a single non-intrusive, privacy-safe sponsored text line and credits 50% of the verified ad revenue directly to the user.

2. Zero Prompt Reading Guarantee

We do not read, transmit, store, or analyze your prompt inputs, model outputs, or conversation history. The extension operates purely on DOM lifecycle events (detecting when an assistant message container is created or streaming). The textual content of your conversations never leaves your browser.

3. Data Collection and Usage

WaitWise adheres to the principle of minimal data collection:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII): None collected. You do not need to create an account or provide an email to use the extension.
  • Browsing History: Never collected. The extension only activates on explicitly declared host permissions (chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com).
  • Local Storage: Stored locally on your device via chrome.storage.local to keep track of your earned cash balance, daily impression count, and extension enabled/paused preference.
  • Impression Verification: When an ad is displayed for the minimum 5.0-second dwell time, an anonymous cryptographic nonce and campaign ID are sent to record the qualified impression for payout verification.

4. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Used strictly to persist your unpaid earnings balance and extension enable/pause preference locally.
  • host_permissions: Restricted solely to supported AI chat web applications to mount the single sponsored line in the active conversation viewport.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising

WaitWise does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Advertisers do not receive user identifiers, browsing habits, or prompt contexts. Advertisers only purchase impression blocks based on aggregate dwell metrics.

6. Contact Information

For questions or concerns regarding this policy, reach out to our team at privacy@waitwise.ai.
